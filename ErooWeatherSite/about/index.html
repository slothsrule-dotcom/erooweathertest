About Eroo Weather

Eroo Weather was founded on April 16, 2026. The original name for Eroo Weather was "Real Weather" because we wanted (and still want to!) provide real, accurate weather forecasts. Later, the name was changed to "Oreo Weather," named after our founder's cat, Oreo. However, the name was eventually changed to "Eroo Weather" due to potential legal challenges with the cookie brand Oreo. Today, our mission remains the same: providing accurate weather information while continuing to expand our services. If you have any suggestions for features or improvements, feel free to email us at contact@erooweather.com.

*Eroo Weather is not affiliated with the cookie brand Oreo in any way.
